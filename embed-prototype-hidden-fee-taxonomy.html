Hidden-Fee Taxonomy

Reference categories for understanding fee presentation. Categories do not establish illegality or deception.

DetectHiddenFees Public Hidden-Fee Taxonomy, version 0.1
CategoryPlain-language meaningQuestion to ask
Disclosed mandatory feeA required charge identified before commitment.Was it included in the total price?
Poorly disclosed mandatory feeA required charge whose timing, label, or amount is unclear.What does it cover and when was it shown?
Optional add-onA charge for an optional product or service.Can I decline it?
Recurring feeA charge that repeats while a service remains active.How often is it charged and how is it canceled?
Contract-triggered feeA charge activated by renewal, cancellation, late payment, or another event.What triggers it and how is it calculated?
