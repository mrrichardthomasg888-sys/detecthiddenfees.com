# Research Page Validation Checklist

- [ ] One canonical URL and one principal authority page.
- [ ] Research question, scope, date range, sample, definitions, and limitations visible.
- [ ] Every result has a source and reproducible method.
- [ ] Data download includes metadata, license, version, and corrections path.
- [ ] Charts have text alternatives and accessible labels.
- [ ] Ownership/funding disclosure is accurate.
- [ ] Author/reviewer identity is verified or organizational.
- [ ] No customer data, fabricated result, legal overclaim, or unsupported product claim.
- [ ] Appropriate schema only.
