# Phase 4 Measurement Framework

Measure authority quality, not vanity volume. Record a dated baseline before publication; do not invent one.

## Search and AI

Track organic impressions/clicks, branded/nonbranded visibility, indexed pages, featured snippets, observable AI Overview appearances, Bing visibility, and citation URL consistency across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Copilot, and other systems where observation is lawful and reproducible.

## Links

Track new referring domains, editorial relevance, destination asset, link type, anchor distribution, research/tool/taxonomy links, lost links, and reclaimed links. Record source page, date, relationship, and whether the link remains live.

## Engagement and quality

Track research visits, downloads, calculator use, embed use, journalist requests, product referrals, corrections, dead citations, outdated claims, reviewer coverage, and owner-confirmation backlog. Report quarterly with methodology and limitations.
