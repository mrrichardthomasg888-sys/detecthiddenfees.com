# Dataset and Statistics Page Standards

An evidence page must include the research question, executive summary, dataset size, date range, sampling and eligibility rules, definitions, methods, results, limitations, funding and conflicts, attribution, sources, correction contact, and last-updated date. Downloadable data is offered only when lawful and privacy-safe. Charts require accessible descriptions and reproducible labels. Suggested citations identify the organization, title, date, and canonical URL.

`Dataset` schema is reserved for an actual accessible dataset with a stable description and distribution. An ordinary article or an unpublished plan does not qualify.
