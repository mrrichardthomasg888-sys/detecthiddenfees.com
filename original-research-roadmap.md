# Original Research Roadmap

These are research proposals, not findings. No results, prevalence rates, savings figures, or benchmarks should be published until the data, sampling, legal/privacy review, and methodology gates are complete.

Priority candidates are the Annual Consumer Fee Index, Contract Clause Risk Report, Fee Disclosure Quality Study, and AI Contract Analysis Benchmark. Each requires a defined question, lawful data source, sampling plan, inclusion/exclusion rules, taxonomy, human verification, limitations, conflict disclosure, correction path, and publication review. Potential outputs include a report, accessible charts, a methodology page, a suggested citation, and a lawful downloadable data file.

Partnerships with universities or nonprofits may improve methods and credibility, but outreach is deferred. A project may begin with existing public data only after verifying terms, date capture, reproducibility, and that the sample supports the proposed claim.
