# Embeddable Asset Policy

Embeds must provide genuine public value, visible attribution, a canonical source link, accessible text equivalent, responsive behavior, a static-image alternative, licensing terms, and a version or update date. Attribution is not required to be followed. Embeds must not inject hidden links, unrelated CTAs, tracking by default, unsafe scripts, or misleading claims. The source page remains authoritative and corrections must propagate through the version notice.
