# Backlink Prospecting Process

1. Start from a finished asset and define the audience it serves.
2. Identify a small set of relevant organizations or public pages.
3. Record only public organizational or role information needed for qualification.
4. Score relevance, legitimacy, audience fit, and spam risk.
5. Check conflicts, commercial relationships, and whether the page accepts educational resources.
6. Draft a specific value proposition; do not send in Phase 4.
7. Limit follow-up to two messages after explicit approval.
8. Record outcomes, corrections, and do-not-contact requests.
