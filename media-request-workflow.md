# Media Request Workflow

1. Record the request, deadline, topic, outlet, and requested claims.
2. Confirm the asset and source support.
3. Route legal, financial, privacy, security, or AI-performance questions to an approved reviewer.
4. Use only approved quotes and verified organizational facts.
5. Disclose ownership and commercial relationships.
6. Record the response, sources, limitations, and correction path.
7. Do not promise exclusivity, links, or publication.
