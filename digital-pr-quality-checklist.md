# Digital PR Quality Checklist

- [ ] No invented result, quote, partnership, expert, or readership claim.
- [ ] Methodology and limitations are public.
- [ ] Data is lawful, anonymized, and reproducible.
- [ ] Primary sources support material claims.
- [ ] Ownership and commercial benefit are disclosed.
- [ ] Charts have accessible descriptions.
- [ ] Suggested citation and correction path exist.
- [ ] Legal/privacy/security/AI review is complete where required.
- [ ] No promise of a followed link or reciprocal placement.
- [ ] Outreach is personalized, limited, and approved.
