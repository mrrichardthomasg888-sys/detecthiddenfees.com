# Expert Contributor Program

Recruitment is future work. Possible reviewers include consumer-protection attorneys, contract attorneys, financial counselors, CPAs, medical-billing advocates, privacy/security professionals, AI evaluators, researchers, and consumer advocates.

Each contributor must agree to the scope, provide verifiable qualifications, disclose conflicts, approve the public description, record review date, and identify what was and was not reviewed. Compensation and ownership relationships are disclosed. A review does not turn educational content into legal, financial, medical, privacy, or security advice.
