# Research Methodology Framework

This framework prepares DetectHiddenFees for future research; it does not report findings.

Each project must state its question, hypothesis or descriptive purpose, eligible source population, inclusion/exclusion criteria, jurisdiction, date range, sampling method, sample-size rationale, fee and clause taxonomy, anonymization and personal-data controls, conflict disclosure, quality-control process, human verification, model and analysis version, false-positive/false-negative review, uncertainty or confidence intervals where appropriate, limitations, reproducibility materials, correction process, retention period, and update schedule.

Customer documents must not be published or used as a research dataset without documented consent, lawful handling, de-identification, access controls, and owner approval. Proprietary prompts and sensitive infrastructure details remain private. Research pages require methodology review before publication and must distinguish observed results from interpretation.
