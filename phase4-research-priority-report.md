# Phase 4 Research Priority Report

The scoring model is a planning tool, not a claim about market results. Scores use the 1–10 factors in `phase4-research-priority-model.json`; high difficulty, cost, legal risk, privacy risk, and verification burden reduce near-term priority.

## Initial flagship sequence

1. **Hidden Fee Transparency Index** — strongest balance of consumer value, journalist interest, recurring update potential, public-data feasibility, and topical authority. Build the methodology and pilot dataset first; publish findings only after manual verification.
2. **Hidden Fee Taxonomy** — low-risk, immediately useful reference asset based on the already approved taxonomy framework. It can become the controlled vocabulary for later research and tools.
3. **Public Contract Clause Library** — strong citation and education value, but entries require cautious wording and legal-source review. Begin with a small set of entries, not mass page creation.

## Deferred projects

The State Hidden-Fee Law Tracker, Contract Clause Risk Report, AI Contract Analysis Benchmark, Medical Billing Fee Study, Consumer Negotiation Survey, and Industry Fee Benchmark Database remain gated by legal review, privacy/consent, verified product evidence, or reliable data. Subscription, rental, telecom, travel, banking, annual-index, and scorecard projects are later candidates after the flagship methodology is proven.

No research has been published and no outreach has been sent in Phase 4.
