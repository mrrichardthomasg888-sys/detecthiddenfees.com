# White-Hat Backlink Strategy

Earn links by publishing useful, reviewable assets: the taxonomy, verified research, public calculators, clause library, accessible data, and journalist resources. Priority audiences are consumer advocates, financial-literacy organizations, libraries, universities, legal-education publishers, personal-finance and industry publications, data journalists, and relevant newsletters.

Every prospect must have topical relevance, a real editorial audience, legitimate publishing activity, a relevant destination, and a specific reason the asset helps readers. Do not buy links, request reciprocal links, use directories or PBNs, submit bulk forms, use guest-post farms, or publish unrelated content. A link is never a condition of a public-interest partnership.
