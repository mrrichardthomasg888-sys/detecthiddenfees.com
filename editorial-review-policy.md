# Editorial Review Policy

Review status reflects work actually completed. A page must not say “expert reviewed,” “legally reviewed,” or “security reviewed” without a real qualified reviewer, a documented scope, and a review date.

| Content | Review | Minimum verification |
|---|---|---|
| General educational guide | Editorial review recommended | Accuracy, clarity, links, scope |
| Legal rights or law | Qualified legal review recommended; mandatory before legal conclusions | Jurisdiction, effective date, exceptions, disclaimer |
| Consumer finance, pricing, tax | Qualified finance/accounting review recommended | Definitions, calculations, dates, assumptions |
| Privacy or security | Privacy/security review mandatory for product behavior claims | Actual policy and implementation, retention, access, controls |
| AI capability or performance | Product/AI technical review mandatory | Test protocol, model/configuration, version, limitations |
| Research or dataset | Methodology review mandatory | Sampling, inclusion, reproducibility, limitations |

Review frequency is annual for stable educational pages and event-driven for laws, prices, policies, or product behavior. If a reviewer is unavailable, the page uses cautious educational wording and does not display a specialist-review badge.
