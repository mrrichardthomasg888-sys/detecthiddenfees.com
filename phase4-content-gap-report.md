# Phase 4 Content-Gap Report

The highest-value gaps are evidence assets and utilities, not mass articles. The recommended sequence is the public taxonomy, a rigorously sampled Hidden Fee Transparency Index, a small reviewed clause library, and one or two input-only calculators. State-law resources remain gated by official-source and legal review. New opportunities must have a clear parent, unique intent, source plan, review requirement, link value, AI-citation value, and cannibalization check.
