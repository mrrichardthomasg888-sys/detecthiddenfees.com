# Outreach Readiness Checklist

No outreach is authorized in Phase 3. Before future white-hat promotion, each asset should have:

- A defensible research question and methodology.
- Real data, lawful use, and documented limitations.
- Expert or methodology review where required.
- Accessible charts and, where lawful, downloadable data.
- Suggested citation and stable canonical URL.
- A clear corrections contact and ownership disclosure.
- A concise press summary and a fact-checked methodology page.
- No unsupported performance, savings, legal, or prevalence claims.

Assets that still need data, review, legal/privacy approval, downloadable files, charts, or a press package remain planning items rather than linkable publications.
