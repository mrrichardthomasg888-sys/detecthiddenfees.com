Hidden Fee Statistics: What the Sample Supports

The 25-record public-source sample is published with sample-only counts and explicit limits; no market-wide percentages are asserted.

View the Data ManifestRead the Methodology

Current result: The public dataset now contains 25 verified source records. It publishes record counts by sample category, but no hidden-fee prevalence, average amount, or market-wide percentage because this reviewed guidance sample is not representative.

Research record

Who: DetectHiddenFees Research Lab.

What: A verified public-source review of fee terminology, contract clauses, cancellation terms, renewal language, and unexpected charges; this is a documented sample, not a representative prevalence study.

When: The 25 sources were collected and reviewed on August 8, 2026. The public manifest was last updated August 8, 2026.

How: A record must retain its source URL, scope, evidence reference, classification, and verification status before it can support a finding.

Data size: The published sample contains 25 verified records. Its category counts describe this sample only; no market-wide prevalence statistic is calculated.

Limitations: A category or example does not establish that a fee is common, unlawful, deceptive, or representative. Inspect the manifest and methodology before citing the status.

Why the numbers are withheld

A defensible market statistic needs a defined population, source set, date range, inclusion rule, denominator, calculation, and limitations. This release therefore publishes sample counts only and does not present them as market estimates.

Future statistics may include

If the evidence supports them, future releases may summarize fee terminology, fee categories, recurring charges, renewal structures, cancellation windows, or industry comparisons. Each result will identify exactly which records support it.

Not a product-performance report

This research page will not publish model precision, recall, OCR performance, savings rates, or customer-outcome claims without a documented test protocol and reviewable evidence. A product page and a research result are different kinds of information.

How to cite this sample analysis

Cite this page as a sample-analysis statement, not as evidence of a hidden-fee prevalence rate. Check the manifest and changelog before relying on a future release.
