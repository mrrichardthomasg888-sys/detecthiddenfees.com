## Selection and withholding rules

The three created pages were the only candidates that simultaneously had a clear unique intent, a low overlap risk, an existing parent, a defensible evidence plan, and enough original educational substance for a full page. No existing successful page was rewritten, merged, deleted, redirected, canonicalized, or noindexed. Pages involving state law, prevalence statistics, proprietary product performance, or legal conclusions remain deferred until their required review gates are complete.
