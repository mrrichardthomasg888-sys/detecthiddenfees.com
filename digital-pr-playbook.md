# Digital PR Playbook

Digital PR begins only after an asset has real data, methodology, review, charts, citation text, ownership disclosure, and a corrections path. Campaign types include an annual index, industry fee study, regulatory explainer, state-law update, taxonomy release, or contract-clause report.

For each campaign: define the trigger, audience, evidence, headline, chart set, supporting guides, expert-review needs, local and national angles, media summary, social assets, disclosure, correction process, and success metrics. Success is measured by relevant editorial references, accurate citations, useful public engagement, and durable links—not volume of outreach or links. Phase 4 sends no outreach.
