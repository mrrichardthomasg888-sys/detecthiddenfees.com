# University Partnership Strategy

Potential formats include financial-literacy curriculum, a student research project, methodology review, public calculator, library guide, legal-clinic resource, or public-interest dataset. Offer genuine educational value and disclose ownership and commercial benefit. Do not request an .edu link as the objective, offer payment for links, claim endorsement, or present a product pitch as research. Any collaboration requires written scope, consent, attribution, review responsibility, and data/privacy terms.
