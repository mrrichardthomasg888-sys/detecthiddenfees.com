# Outreach Template Library

All templates are future-use drafts. No message is sent without owner approval and a completed, reviewed asset.

## Research launch

Subject: A public resource for [audience/topic]

Hello [name],

I’m sharing [specific asset] because your [specific page/program] helps [audience] understand [topic]. The resource includes [methodology/data/limitations] and a suggested citation. If it is useful to your readers, you may review it here: [URL]. No link or response is expected.

Best,  
[verified sender and relationship disclosure]

## Resource-page suggestion

Hello [name], your [page] covers [topic]. Our [asset] addresses [specific gap] using [source/method]. Please review it only if it fits your editorial standards: [URL]. DetectHiddenFees is related to HiddenFeeAI; any commercial relationship is disclosed on the asset.

## University/nonprofit resource

Hello [name], we prepared [asset] as a public educational resource for [audience]. We are seeking methodological feedback or educational use, not a link or endorsement. Ownership and any commercial benefit are disclosed here: [URL]. Would this be relevant for your review process?

## Data-journalist pitch

Hello [name], [asset] examines [question] using [sample/date/method]. The downloadable data and limitations are available at [URL]. The main result is [verified result only]. I can provide methodology clarification; I will not speculate beyond the published evidence.

## Follow-up

Hello [name], following up once on [asset] in case it is relevant to [specific page/topic]. No action is needed if it is not a fit. I won’t follow up again unless you reply.

## Thank you / correction

Thank you for reviewing [asset]. If you notice an error or outdated source, please use [correction path]. We will investigate and record material corrections transparently.
