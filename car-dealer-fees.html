Automotive hub FAQs

What is the fastest way to use the automotive hub?

Start with the guide that matches the document or conversation in front of you, then compare the written out-the-door total, APR, amount financed, and total of payments.

Are dealer add-ons required?

Many dealer add-ons are optional. Ask the dealer to identify any claimed legal or lender requirement in writing and request a clean quote without optional products.

What should I review before signing?

Review the vehicle price, every fee, trade value and payoff, selected products, APR, term, payment, amount financed, finance charge, total of payments, and whether delivery is final.

Can HiddenFeeAI review car-buying documents?

HiddenFeeAI can help surface fees, add-ons, financing risks, cancellation language, mismatched numbers, and negotiation opportunities in uploaded documents.

What if a dealer changes the terms after delivery?

Do not sign a replacement contract without comparing it line by line with the original. Preserve every document and ask for the financing condition and deadline in writing.
