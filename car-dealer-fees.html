Automotive hub FAQs

Where should I start?

Choose the guide that matches the document or conversation in front of you, then compare written totals and terms.

What documents can HiddenFeeAI review?

Quotes, purchase agreements, auto-loan contracts, product agreements, and related paperwork can be organized for a second set of eyes.

Are dealer add-ons required?

Many add-ons are optional; ask for the claimed legal or lender requirement in writing and request a clean quote.

What should I compare besides payment?

Compare selling price, fees, APR, amount financed, term, finance charge, and total of payments.

What if financing changes after delivery?

Preserve the signed packet, ask for written lender information, and do not sign replacement terms without comparing them.
