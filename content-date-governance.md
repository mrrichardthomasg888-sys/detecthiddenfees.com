# Content Date Governance

Dates describe content work, not automated file touches. `datePublished` is used only when the original publication date is known. `dateModified`, visible “Last updated,” sitemap `lastmod`, and RSS dates change only after a material factual, source, legal, pricing, product, or methodology update. Navigation, styling, grammar, and metadata-only changes do not reset the material date.

“Last reviewed” is shown only when a documented editorial or specialist review occurred. A review records reviewer identity or organizational role, scope, date, sources checked, and unresolved limitations. Future dates and uniform dates across unrelated pages are prohibited.

For volatile pages, review at least annually and after a relevant law, regulator guidance, price, product policy, or capability change. If the underlying date cannot be defended, omit the field rather than invent it. Date changes are recorded in the repository change history and, where appropriate, a page revision note.
