# Flagship Project Specification: Hidden Fee Transparency Index

**Status:** Specification and pilot design only. No findings are asserted.

## Purpose

Measure how clearly selected public prices present required, optional, recurring, conditional, and contract-triggered charges before a consumer commits. The index evaluates disclosure quality, not whether a company or fee is illegal.

## Research question and value

How consistently do selected providers present total prices and fee triggers across defined industries, dates, and purchase paths? The asset would give consumers a practical comparison framework, give journalists a documented data source, and give educators a reusable explanation of fee disclosure.

## Scope and sampling

Begin with no more than three industries and a published inclusion list. Select public pricing or contract pages using a documented rule, capture the date and URL, and record only information lawfully available to the public. Exclude private accounts, customer documents, paywalled material, pages requiring prohibited automation, and transactions that cannot be reproduced.

## Definitions and scoring

Record base price, mandatory charges, optional add-ons, automatic selections, recurring charges, conditional charges, taxes, disclosure timing, label clarity, total-price availability, cancellation/renewal visibility, and source evidence. Score each dimension with a pre-registered 0–2 rubric. Publish raw observations, rationale, date, and limitations; do not convert the score into a legal conclusion or universal company ranking without review.

## Quality control

Two-person review for every scored observation; disagreements are adjudicated and logged. Recheck a sample after publication. Preserve screenshots or permitted archives, source URLs, capture dates, rubric version, and corrections history. A methodology reviewer must approve the rubric before results are public.

## Legal, privacy, and conflicts

Use public pages only, respect terms and rate limits, avoid personal data, and do not imply endorsement or wrongdoing. Disclose DetectHiddenFees/HiddenFeeAI ownership and commercial benefit once owner-confirmed. Obtain legal review before using language about deceptive pricing, legal compliance, or regulated industries.

## Publication and linkable assets

Publish one canonical research page, a methodology page, accessible charts, a CSV/JSON dataset with metadata, a PDF summary, a suggested citation, and a static chart image. Provide a responsive embed with attribution and a text alternative. Update annually only if the same rubric and sampling process can be repeated.

**Suggested citation:** DetectHiddenFees Editorial Team. *Hidden Fee Transparency Index: [edition and scope].* DetectHiddenFees.com, [publication date], [canonical URL].

**Success metrics:** verified citations, relevant editorial links, dataset downloads, corrections resolved, repeatability, and useful consumer engagement—not raw backlink count.
