# Resource-Page Link Acquisition Strategy

Match each finished asset to legitimate pages about consumer rights, financial literacy, contract education, rental, travel, medical billing, subscriptions, small-business contracts, pricing transparency, and auto-renewal. The asset must add something specific, be non-deceptive, disclose commercial relationships, and meet the page owner’s editorial standards. Do not force a product CTA into a public-interest page. A resource may be declined when it is promotional, unsupported, stale, or too broad.
