# Hidden-Fee Taxonomy Framework

This framework is an organizing system for future guides, research, tools, and internal links. Inclusion does not mean a charge is hidden, illegal, deceptive, or improper. The page and document context determine that question.

## Core dimensions

- **Disclosure:** disclosed, late-disclosed, unclear, or absent.
- **Trigger:** recurring, usage-based, cancellation, renewal, event, or discretionary.
- **Presentation:** standalone line item, bundled amount, pass-through, tax, or adjustment.
- **Evidence:** quote, contract, invoice, checkout screen, notice, or account history.
- **Action:** ask for itemization, compare total price, verify rights, dispute an error, or seek professional advice.

Initial categories are defined in `hidden-fee-taxonomy.json`: mandatory disclosed fees, recurring fees, cancellation fees, administrative/processing fees, price escalation or variable charges, and duplicate/unclear charges. Future categories such as delivery, activation, installation, renewal, minimum-spend, pass-through, taxes mislabeled as fees, and dynamic pricing must use the same definition, evidence, limitations, and detection fields before publication.
