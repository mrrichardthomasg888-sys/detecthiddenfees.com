# Journalist Resource Center Specification

The resource center should contain the organization overview, mission, verified ownership relationship, approved logos, brand usage, research methodology, current research assets, lawful downloads, suggested citations, correction path, media-request path, approved quotes, licensing, and commercial disclosure. It must not contain invented coverage, spokesperson credentials, readership figures, partnerships, or statistics. Until ownership and spokesperson details are confirmed, publish a framework rather than a press-facing factual profile.
