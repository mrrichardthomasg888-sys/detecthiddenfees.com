# Contract-Clause Taxonomy Framework

The taxonomy supports education and future document-analysis evaluation. It does not label clauses abusive or illegal. A clause may serve a legitimate business purpose, and its effect depends on the full agreement, facts, negotiation, and jurisdiction.

The initial JSON framework covers automatic renewal, price escalation, unilateral modification, termination/cancellation, auto-billing authorization, and limitation of liability. Future additions—arbitration, class-action waiver, indemnification, warranty disclaimer, personal guarantee, governing law, venue, data-use permission, late fees, collection costs, acceleration, assignment, exclusivity, intellectual property, audit rights, and refund restrictions—must document wording patterns, purpose, risks, questions, review needs, and limitations.
