# State-Law Review Checklist

- [ ] Official source opened and archived or recorded.
- [ ] State and jurisdiction match the claim.
- [ ] Enacted/effective/proposed/repealed status verified.
- [ ] Effective date and exceptions recorded.
- [ ] Covered industries and transaction type stated.
- [ ] Private-right and penalty wording verified, not inferred.
- [ ] Attorney-general or regulator guidance checked.
- [ ] Qualified legal review completed.
- [ ] Current-law warning and disclaimer visible.
- [ ] Revision history and next review date recorded.
