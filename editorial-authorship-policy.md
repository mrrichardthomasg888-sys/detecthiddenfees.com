# Editorial Authorship Policy

DetectHiddenFees uses transparent authorship. A real, verified individual may be named only when the project owner has supplied or published enough information to support the person’s identity, role, and relevant qualifications. No credentials, licenses, employers, or experience are inferred.

When no verified individual is available, the byline is **DetectHiddenFees Editorial Team**. Organizational authorship describes editorial responsibility; it does not imply legal, financial, medical, security, or research credentials.

Author profiles must state the person’s real role, supported areas of contribution, verified credentials if relevant, legitimate profile links, and conflicts of interest. Corrections to authorship are documented. Former contributors are identified accurately rather than silently replaced. Product ownership or commercial relationships are disclosed on relevant pages.

Person schema is used only for a visible, verifiable profile. Article schema author values must match the visible byline. Reviewers are not listed unless they actually reviewed the page and the review scope and date can be documented.
